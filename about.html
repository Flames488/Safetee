Our story

Why we started

Every day, families face uncertainty — assault, unsafe commutes, missing persons and emergencies happen in communities everywhere. In too many situations, help arrives too late.

We realised that lives could be saved if loved ones and responders were alerted instantly, with accurate information — location, context and a direct line to the people who care most.

That's why we built SAFETEE — an intelligent safety platform designed to cut emergency response time and give people the peace of mind they deserve.
